About

I’ve worked in software since I was fifteen: first as an engineer, then running development teams, including as Head of Application Development and Support at the RSPB. For the last several years I’ve worked as an agile and systems coach, helping organisations and the people in them through change.

So I know what it’s like to be the person in the middle: the one carrying a decision made elsewhere to a team who didn’t ask for it.
